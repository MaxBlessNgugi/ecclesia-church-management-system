# ECCLESIA ChMS — Pricing Proposal

> Suggested commercial structure. All prices in KSh and indicative — adjust for
> your market and the parish's budget. The local-first model is the key selling
> point: **no ongoing SaaS fees, data stays on the parish PC.**

---

## Option A — One-time License + Annual Support *(recommended)*

| Item | Price |
|------|-------|
| One-time software license (per parish) | KSh 35,000 |
| First-year setup, data migration & training | KSh 15,000 |
| Annual support & updates (from year 2) | KSh 10,000 / year |

**What's included**
- Installer-run setup on the parish's Windows PC (Node.js LTS)
- Up to 2 staff trained (registry, finance, reports)
- Import of existing Excel/paper member data (export/import tool)
- Email/WhatsApp support, bug fixes and minor updates
- Automatic backups configured (local + optional cloud mirror)

---

## Option B — Multi-Station License *(parish network)*

| Item | Price |
|------|-------|
| Base license (main PC) | KSh 35,000 |
| Additional stations (up to 3 more PCs on the parish LAN) | KSh 10,000 each |
| Annual support & updates | KSh 15,000 / year |

> One shared database on the main PC; staff on other PCs access it over the
> parish LAN. Ideal for parishes with a secretary, treasurer and sacristan
> working at the same time.

---

## Option C — Trial / Evaluation *(for pitching)*

| Item | Price |
|------|-------|
| 30-day fully functional trial (demo data included) | Free |
| Post-trial conversion | Deduct nothing — full license at Option A |

> The trial is the full product pre-loaded with realistic sample data so the
> parish council can explore every module. Data from the trial is wiped at
> handover (one command) before real records are entered.

---

## What the parish pays for — the honest list

| They get | You deliver |
|----------|-------------|
| Software | License + installer + updates |
| Set-up | Install, configure backups, import existing data |
| People | Staff training session + documentation |
| Safety | Automatic backups, off-site copy option, audit trail |
| Support | A named contact for questions and fixes |

---

## What they do NOT pay for

- No per-user monthly fee
- No hosting bill (runs on their own PC)
- No internet required to work
- No data migration lock-in — full export included in the product

---

## Payment terms (suggested)

1. **50% deposit** to schedule setup and begin data migration.
2. **50% on go-live** (after training and successful sign-off).
3. **Annual support** invoiced each anniversary of go-live.
